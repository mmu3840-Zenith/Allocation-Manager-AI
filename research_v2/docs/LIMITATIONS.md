# Limitations and Research Integrity

The current environment is a computational abstraction, not a physical
robotics simulator.

No claims of physical robot validation, real warehouse deployment,
global optimality, or real-world throughput are supported.

The composite objective is an exploratory score. Component metrics
must be interpreted separately.

The experiment does not model:
- Spatial navigation or collisions.
- Communication delays or packet loss.
- Energy consumption.
- Online task arrivals.
- Worker recovery.
- Dynamic reassignment.
- Physical robot hardware.

All numerical claims in publications must be calculated from the
generated experiment outputs and independently checked.
